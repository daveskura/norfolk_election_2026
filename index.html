About this project

Campaign promises, made easy to compare.

Every election, voters are asked to make an important decision with scattered information: a flyer here, a debate clip there, a website that may or may not answer the questions you care about. This project brings it all into one place.

Each mayoral candidate's publicly available campaign material will be gathered and given to four AI models: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek. Each model is asked the same questions about every candidate, and the results are laid out in a single comparison chart. Where the models agree, you can read with confidence. Where they differ, you'll see that too.

Alongside the analysis, each candidate will have a short, factual biography so you know who they are before you read what they propose.

What you'll find here

  • A one-paragraph bio for every mayoral candidate
  • Key platform positions, summarized in plain language
  • A comparison chart across all candidates and all four AI models
  • Links back to each candidate's own campaign sources

How it works

One method, applied to everyone.

The same process is used for every candidate, so no one gets a different set of questions or a friendlier summary.

Gather

Collect each candidate's public platform, website, and published statements.

Ask

Give every AI model the same material and the same set of questions.

Compare

Line up the answers side by side, by candidate and by issue.

Publish

Share the results with sources, so you can check anything yourself.

Our commitments

Built to inform, not persuade.

Nonpartisan

This site does not endorse any candidate and is not affiliated with any campaign or party. Every candidate is treated the same way.

Transparent

The questions asked, the source material used, and each model's answers will be published so anyone can see how the results were produced.

Verify for yourself

AI models can make mistakes. Use this guide as a starting point, and check the candidates' own words before you vote.

Are you a candidate, or spot something to fix?

Candidates are welcome to share their official platform materials, and anyone can send a correction. Accuracy matters more than anything here.
